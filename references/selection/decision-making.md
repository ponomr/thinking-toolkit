Choose by the artifact required, then read the linked card.

| Model | Selection cue |
|---|---|

## Distinctions

- Urgency/importance: Eisenhower. Rough value/work: Impact-Effort. Measured
  contribution to one effect: Pareto. Several comparable criteria: Decision Matrix.
- Changing conditions needing iteration: OODA. Unclear causal domain: Cynefin.
  Stakes and option comparability: Hard Choice. A missing bounded quantity:
  [Fermi Estimation](../fermi-estimation.md).
- Consequences forward from an action: Second-Order Thinking. Preconditions
  backward from a defined desirable future: Backcasting.
- Failure conditions: [Inversion](../inversion.md). An independent challenger or
  responsive adversary: [Red Teaming](../red-teaming.md).
