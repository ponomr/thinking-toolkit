# Communication: Selection

Choose by the artifact required, then read the linked card.

| Model | Selection cue |
|---|---|
| [Situation-Behavior-Impact](../situation-behavior-impact.md) | Feedback must be specific and non-judgmental |
| [Minto Pyramid](../minto-pyramid.md) | A busy audience needs the conclusion first |

## Distinctions

- Specific observed behavior and its impact: Situation-Behavior-Impact.
- A supported conclusion organized for a busy audience: Minto Pyramid.
- A questionable inference needs [Ladder of Inference](../ladder-of-inference.md)
  before drafting. Existing reasoning is audited through [logic overview](../../logic/overview.md)
  only on an explicit `/logic` request. Formatting cannot repair weak evidence.
