# Systems thinking: Selection

Choose by the artifact required, then read the linked card.

| Model | Selection cue |
|---|---|
| [Iceberg Model](../iceberg-model.md) | Repeated events point to deeper system causes |
| [Connection Circles](../connection-circles.md) | Variables and feedback relationships need mapping |
| [Concept Map](../concept-map.md) | Concepts and their semantic relationships need clarity |
| [Balancing Feedback Loop](../balancing-feedback-loop.md) | A system resists change or seeks a target |
| [Reinforcing Feedback Loop](../reinforcing-feedback-loop.md) | Growth or decline feeds further growth or decline |

## Distinctions

- Semantic propositions between concepts: Concept Map. Directional causal
  influences between changing variables: Connection Circles.
- Goal-seeking or counteracting closed path: Balancing Feedback Loop.
  Self-amplifying closed path: Reinforcing Feedback Loop. A trend alone does
  not establish a loop; inspect the closed causal path.
- Events recurring through patterns, structures, and mental models: Iceberg.
  One incident's causal chain: [Five Whys](../five-whys.md). One completed
  episode's intended/actual gap: [After Action Review](../after-action-review.md).
